Common questions

Where do I find my weekly plan?

Open your PlanPulse dashboard. Your new plan appears on Sunday at 7 AM ET with a schedule you can follow throughout the week.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Eligible new subscribers can try Plus ($15/mo) or All-Access ($19/mo) free for 7 days through Stripe. After the trial, the monthly subscription starts unless you cancel before the trial ends.

Can I switch tracks or adjust my preferences?

Yes. Update your track, goals, or available days in your dashboard by Saturday at noon ET for the following week.
